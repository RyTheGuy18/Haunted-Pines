HOW TO SURVIVE
Find 3 fuses, bring them to the breaker, then find the exit door.

Hide in lockers. It can't open them, but hold your breath when it's close.
Blind it: hold your flashlight on its face up close to stun it.
Escape Level 0 and everyone still alive drops into Level 1, a dark parking garage. Start all 5 generators (it takes a while and it's LOUD: the Occupant will come), then find the exit.
Escape the garage and you wake up in Level 37, the Poolrooms. Turn all 3 drain valves to empty the great pool, then climb down to the drain at the bottom.
Adrenaline kicks in when it's close, so run. It walks faster than you but it can't sprint.
Crouch in the dark with your light off and it can barely see you.
Trust nobody far away. It can look like one of you. Watch for a hazmat suit with no flashlight and lights flickering around it, or hold your light on them.

The Occupant hunts by sound: it hears running, talking and gasps, but can't see you through walls. It can kill the lights, move the walls, fake footsteps, disguise itself as a survivor, lunge forward with its arms out, and crawl through vents (you'll hear it climb out). It hears every fuse you place.
When the computer hunts you, pick how smart it is: EASY barely listens, IMPOSSIBLE reads where you're going and cuts you off, but it still can't outrun you and your flashlight still works on it.

iPhone: tap Share → Add to Home Screen and play from there. It runs fullscreen with no browser bars and the controls work much better.
